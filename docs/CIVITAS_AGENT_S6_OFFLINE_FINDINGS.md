## Interpretation

This pilot establishes engineering execution only. It is **one synthetic
world**, with a researcher-defined (not real market-estimated) latent
fundamental. The approximately 8.2%-8.3% deviations are *not* real market
mispricing estimates. Between-arm differences are descriptive, not
statistically significant results.

**The decisive observation is inadequate two-sided quote coverage.** The
draft pilot quality gate required at least 10% of steps to have valid
two-sided quotes. All three arms were at only 4–5%. Accordingly, the
S6 scorecard correctly flagged **all 3/3** arms as failing. No attempt has
been made to silently label one-sided books as zero-spread, interpolate
missing quotes, inject artificial transactions, or force the market price
toward the latent benchmark.

### Gate before starting expensive LLM experiments

Do **not** scale B3–B7 on this exact default market configuration. First
run a development-only microstructure sensitivity study while *holding
agent policy, world seed, shock, and accounting rules fixed*, varying
explicitly declared market assumptions such as the order time-to-live,
limit price aggressiveness and available natural counterparties.

Report per scenario:
- valid bid/ask coverage, order rejection/cancellation, active-trade share,
  stale transaction prices and spread conditional on two-sided quotes;
- distortions from any added genuine liquidity supplier and its information
  advantage if such a policy is ever introduced;
- sensitivity of synthetic price deviation to those structural assumptions.

**Do not tune on held-out worlds.** Freeze selected assumptions after
development and validation, then re-evaluate on untouched worlds. Even
passing a diagnostic gate does not establish a realistic market. Later,
use appropriately sourced and licensed market data for external
validation. This negative pilot is a necessary limitation to preserve in
the manuscript.
